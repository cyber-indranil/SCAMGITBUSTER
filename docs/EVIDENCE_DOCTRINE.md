# Evidence Doctrine and Classification Limits

## Foundation

1. **Discovery is not accusation.** Keyword matches are investigative leads.
2. **Availability is not culpability.** An HTTP 404 may have multiple causes and alone does not prove deletion or criminal conduct.
3. **Integrity is not authenticity of meaning.** A matching SHA-256 supports byte integrity of captured material, not the truth of every statement found inside it.
4. **Static evidence is not runtime proof.** A source-code pattern may be dormant, benign in context, deliberately misleading or incomplete.
5. **Absence of an indicator is not proof of safety.** Limited inspections must say exactly what was and was not reviewed.
6. **Technical linkage is not actor attribution.** Shared hashes, domains, names or templates do not necessarily show common ownership or intent.
7. **Human review remains essential.** Escalation requires independent corroboration, preservation and lawful investigative procedures.

## Practical assessment labels

| Label | Evidentiary meaning | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|
| High-risk artifact match | Known observation correlated with a previously assessed artifact | Not proof of repository intent |
| Suspicious indicators | One or more contextual technical observations require review | Not a criminality determination |
| Insufficient evidence | Analysis did not establish relevant behavior, or coverage is lacking | Must not be called benign |
| No adverse indicators observed | Restricted description of a sufficiently examined scope | Not a clean certificate; not appropriate for filename-only review |
| Current retrieval unavailable | Provider endpoint did not furnish requested content | Not automatically deleted, malicious or exculpatory |

## Report reproducibility

An investigator should be able to identify the scope, retrieval time, repository identity, commit pin (if available), path, preservation hash, rules triggered, result, errors and limitations. Reassessment should not silently rewrite the historical event.

## Legal use

SCAMGITBUSTER outputs are **working investigative intelligence**. They are not stand-alone expert conclusions or legal findings. Applicable statutory electronic-evidence requirements, chain of custody, independent source verification and testimony remain distinct obligations.
